# Feast or Fiend — 剧情与对话设计 / Story & Dialogue Design

**风格基调：** 开罗游戏的轻快循环感（经营→探索→解锁→升级）+ 星露谷的温暖小镇人情味（NPC有日常、有吐槽、有成长）。主角是沉默型经营者（类似星露谷农夫），通过NPC对话和旁白推进。

## 一、世界观与主线剧情

### 背景设定

"调味镇"（Seasoning Town）曾以"万味汇聚"闻名，镇中心有一口传说中能复制任何味道的"原味锅"（The Primordial Pot）。多年前一场"大失味"（The Great Blanding）让全镇食谱失传，原味锅熄火，镇子衰落。

主角继承了一间破旧小餐馆——"最后一勺"（The Last Spoon），位于镇口。开局只有一口锅、一张桌、一桶水。

### 主线三幕

#### 第一幕：水与种子

- 主角只能给客人上水，客人礼貌吐槽
- 去镇外"野味草原"打生菜、胡萝卜小怪，掉落种子
- 解锁菜园，种出食材，第一次做出"普通菜谱"
- 镇民开始回流，餐馆有了人气

#### 第二幕：Boss与菜谱

- 普通菜谱上浮现"boss所在地"提示
- 探索地图击败食物boss，获得高价菜谱
- 发现boss可喂食材强化/变异，掉落对应食谱
- 引入boss社交关系：蛋糕+茶=下午茶套餐组合技；某些boss互相敌对
- 黑暗料理机制登场：喂错食材→狂化boss→掉落黑暗料理

#### 第三幕：原味锅重燃

- 集齐足够菜谱后，原味锅苏醒，要求"最后一宴"
- 最终boss是"大失味"本体——一个由所有失败料理残渣组成的怪物
- 用正常菜谱喂它=强化，用黑暗料理喂它=削弱
- 击败后原味锅重燃，镇子复兴，进入自由经营+全boss图鉴收集

## 二、主要NPC与对话

### 1. 老锅匠·葛雷（Grey the Potsmith）

餐馆隔壁的铁匠，修锅的。毒舌但热心。

**初次见面：**

葛雷："你就是新来的？这锅锈得能当古董卖。……别哭，我修。修好了你请我喝碗汤——哦对，你只会倒水。"

**第一次做出普通菜后：**

葛雷："闻到了。总算不是白开水味。小子，锅是有脾气的，你喂它什么，它记什么。"

**解锁boss提示后：**

葛雷："菜谱背面有字？那是老锅匠的规矩——好味道都藏在危险的地方。去吧，别死，死了没人给我修锅。"

### 2. 镇长·玛德琳（Madeline the Mayor）

优雅、话多、爱吃。推动主线。

**开局：**

玛德琳："亲爱的，欢迎来到调味镇。曾经这里一条街有十七种香味……现在只有你门口那桶水。别灰心，水也是味道的一种——最淡的那种。"

**第一次卖菜后：**

玛德琳："我尝到了……希望？不，是盐放多了。但希望的部分是真的。"

**黑暗料理卖给顾客后（恶评事件）：**

玛德琳："有人投诉吃了你的菜做了三天噩梦。……我尝了一口，确实。但我没投诉，因为噩梦里有我死去的奶奶的炖菜味。奇怪吧？"

### 3. 菜园少女·萝拉（Lola the Gardener）

元气，负责菜园教学。

**解锁菜园：**

萝拉："种子？野外的生菜怪掉的？你打它它掉种子？……这世界真奇妙。来，我教你种。土不会骗人，你给它什么，它还你什么。"

**首次丰收：**

萝拉："看！胡萝卜！……虽然长得像手指。但味道是胡萝卜。种植的秘诀就是——别盯着看，它会害羞。"

### 4. 美食评论家·奥利弗（Oliver the Critic）

挑剔，后期可变成盟友。

**初次光临（只有水）：**

奥利弗："一杯水。室温。杯壁有水痕。……一星。不，半星。那半星给杯子，它至少是干净的。"

**吃到高价菜后：**

奥利弗："……我收回半星。这道菜有野心。野心是好的调料，只要别放太多。"

**吃到黑暗料理后：**

奥利弗："我职业生涯第一次不知道该写什么。它……它在看我。菜在看我。我要给它写一篇五千字的差评，然后偷偷再来吃一次。"

### 5. 下午茶组合·蛋糕夫人与茶先生（Lady Cake & Mr. Tea）

两个boss，社交关系核心。

**蛋糕夫人（野外遭遇）：**

蛋糕夫人："哦？一个厨子。你想喂我草莓？亲爱的，我早就是草莓味了——开玩笑的，来，喂吧，我想变美。"

**茶先生（野外遭遇）：**

茶先生："冷静，年轻人。烫的东西要慢慢喝，急的boss要慢慢打。……不过你看起来挺急的。"

**两者同场触发组合技（旁白）：**

【蛋糕夫人与茶先生相视一笑。空气里飘起伯爵茶香与奶油甜味——"下午茶套餐·双人舞"发动！】

**喂蛋糕夫人草莓后：**

蛋糕夫人："草莓蛋糕！我现在是草莓蛋糕！茶先生你看——（茶先生：看到了，很甜。）"

### 6. 敌对组合·饺子怪与意大利面怪（Dumpling & Spaghetti）

关系不好，同场优先互殴。

**饺子怪（遭遇）：**

饺子怪："肉丸！接住！（扔）……你躲什么？这是待客之道！"

**意大利面怪（遭遇）：**

意大利面怪："面条弹幕，发射！（哗——）别怪我，我天生就散。"

**两者同场（旁白）：**

【饺子怪和意大利面怪一见面就吵了起来——"你皮太厚！""你太散！"——它们决定先解决彼此，暂时忘了你。】

### 7. 寿司怪（Sushi）

用身上的鱼当刀。

**遭遇：**

寿司怪："（拔下身上的鱼）这把刀，是我最好的部分。（挥）——别躲，这是艺术。"

**喂错食材狂化后：**

寿司怪："（鱼刀变黑）……你喂了我什么？……我不再是寿司了。我是……问题。"

### 8. 黑暗料理受害者·小胖（Chubby the Regular）

常客，黑暗料理的"试吃员"。

**吃到黑暗料理：**

小胖："我……我看见我太奶了。她在向我招手。……再来一口，我想问问她菜谱。"

## 三、关键系统对话/旁白

### 喂boss食材时

**正确食材：**

【{boss名}吸收了{食材}，形态发生变化——{新形态名}！它看起来更强了，但也很开心。】

**错误食材（狂化）：**

【{boss名}吞下了{食材}……它的颜色变了。空气变冷了。它不再是食物，而是……饥饿本身。】

### 获得黑暗料理时

【你获得了"黑暗料理"。它在你手里微微颤动。它想被吃掉。它也想被喂给别的boss。】

### 喂黑暗料理给boss时

【{boss名}吃下黑暗料理，气势减弱了。但它看你的眼神里，多了一丝……理解？】

### 卖给顾客黑暗料理时

【顾客吃了一口，沉默了。然后他站起来，鞠了一躬，走了。你收到一条差评——和一笔小费。】

### 原味锅苏醒（第三幕）

【锅底亮起微光。一个苍老的声音：'你喂了锅这么多味道……现在，轮到我喂你了。'】

### 最终boss"大失味"登场

【它由所有失败料理的残渣组成。它没有脸，但你知道它在哭。它说：'我只是……想被尝一口。'】

## 四、结局与后日谈

### 主线结局

【原味锅重燃。第一缕香气飘出时，全镇的人都停下了手里的活。玛德琳哭了，葛雷骂了句脏话，萝拉把胡萝卜扔向了天空。你站在"最后一勺"门口，锅里咕嘟作响。你尝了一口——是家的味道。或者说，是你刚刚创造的味道。】

### 后日谈（自由经营）

- 奥利弗出版《黑暗料理鉴赏指南》，成为畅销书
- 小胖开了"噩梦食堂"分店
- 蛋糕夫人和茶先生在你餐馆办婚礼，菜单是下午茶套餐
- 饺子怪和意大利面怪还在吵，但会一起来吃饭
- 寿司怪偶尔来，安静地坐着，点一杯水

## 五、纯英文版本（English Version）

### World & Main Story

Seasoning Town once famed for "all flavors converging," home to the legendary Primordial Pot. The Great Blanding erased its recipes. You inherit The Last Spoon, a rundown diner at the town gate. One pot, one table, one bucket of water.

**Act I: Water & Seeds** — Serve water, get polite complaints. Beat lettuce/carrot mobs in the Wildflavor Plains for seeds. Unlock the garden. First real recipe. Townsfolk return.

**Act II: Bosses & Recipes** — Recipes reveal boss locations. Defeat food bosses for premium recipes. Feed bosses ingredients to mutate them. Boss social dynamics: Cake + Tea = Afternoon Tea combo; rivals fight each other. Dark Dish mechanic: wrong ingredient → enraged boss → Dark Dish drop.

**Act III: Relight the Pot** — The Primordial Pot awakens, demands "The Last Feast." Final boss: The Great Blanding itself—a monster of failed-dish residue. Feed it normal dishes to strengthen, Dark Dishes to weaken. Victory relights the pot. Free-play + boss compendium.

### Key NPCs

**Grey the Potsmith (sarcastic blacksmith next door)**

First meeting: "You're the new one? This pot's rusty enough to sell as an antique. …Don't cry. I'll fix it. Then you owe me soup—oh right, you only pour water."

After first dish: "Smelled it. Finally, not plain water. Kid, pots have tempers. What you feed it, it remembers."

**Madeline the Mayor (elegant, talkative, food-loving)**

Opening: "Welcome to Seasoning Town, dear. Once seventeen smells on one street… now just your water bucket. Don't lose heart—water is a flavor too. The blandest one."

After Dark Dish complaint: "Someone says your dish gave them three nights of nightmares. …I took a bite. It did. But I didn't complain—the nightmare smelled like my late grandmother's stew. Strange, isn't it?"

**Lola the Gardener (energetic, garden tutorial)**

Unlock garden: "Seeds? From lettuce mobs? You hit it and it drops seeds? …What a world. I'll teach you. Soil doesn't lie. Give it something, it gives back."

First harvest: "Look! Carrots! …They look like fingers. But they taste like carrots. The secret to gardening—don't stare. They get shy."

**Oliver the Critic (picky, later ally)**

First visit (water only): "A glass of water. Room temperature. Smudged rim. …One star. No, half. The half is for the glass. At least it's clean."

After premium dish: "…I take back the half star. This dish has ambition. Ambition is a fine seasoning—just don't overdo it."

After Dark Dish: "First time in my career I don't know what to write. It's… watching me. The dish is watching me. I'll write a five-thousand-word pan, then sneak back for seconds."

**Lady Cake & Mr. Tea (boss duo, social core)**

Lady Cake: "Oh? A chef. You want to feed me strawberries? Darling, I've been strawberry-flavored for years—kidding. Go on, feed me. I want to be prettier."

Mr. Tea: "Easy, young one. Hot things are sipped slowly, angry bosses fought slowly. …You look like you're in a hurry."

Combo trigger: 【Lady Cake and Mr. Tea exchange a smile. Earl Grey and cream fill the air—"Afternoon Tea Duet" activates!】

**Dumpling & Spaghetti (rivals, fight each other)**

Dumpling: "Meatball! Catch! …Why dodge? That's hospitality!"

Spaghetti: "Noodle barrage, fire! Don't blame me—I'm naturally scattered."

Rival encounter: 【They start arguing on sight—"Your skin's too thick!" "You're too loose!"—and forget about you for now.】

**Sushi (uses its fish as a blade)**

Encounter: "（Draws the fish from its body）This blade is my best part. （Slashes）—Don't dodge. This is art."

After wrong ingredient: "（Fish blade turns black）…What did you feed me? …I'm no longer sushi. I'm… a problem."

**Chubby the Regular (Dark Dish taste-tester)**

After Dark Dish: "I… I see my great-grandma. She's waving. …One more bite, I want to ask her for the recipe."

### System Flavor Text

Correct feed: 【{Boss} absorbs {ingredient}, transforms—{new form}! Stronger, but happy.】

Wrong feed (enrage): 【{Boss} swallows {ingredient}… its color shifts. The air turns cold. It's no longer food—it's hunger itself.】

Get Dark Dish: 【You obtained "Dark Dish." It trembles in your hand. It wants to be eaten. It wants to be fed to other bosses.】

Feed Dark Dish: 【{Boss} eats the Dark Dish. Its aura weakens. But its eyes hold a trace of… understanding?】

Sell Dark Dish: 【The customer takes a bite, goes silent. Then stands, bows, and leaves. You get a bad review—and a tip.】

Pot awakens: 【Light flickers at the pot's bottom. An old voice: "You've fed the pot so many flavors… now it's my turn to feed you."】

Final boss: 【It's made of failed-dish residue. No face, but you know it's crying. It says: "I just… wanted to be tasted."】

### Ending

【The Primordial Pot relights. When the first aroma rises, the whole town stops. Madeline cries. Grey swears. Lola throws a carrot at the sky. You stand at The Last Spoon's door, the pot bubbling. You take a sip—it tastes like home. Or rather, the flavor you just created.】

**Post-game:**

- Oliver publishes A Guide to Dark Cuisine (bestseller).
- Chubby opens "Nightmare Diner."
- Lady Cake and Mr. Tea hold their wedding at your restaurant (menu: Afternoon Tea Set).
- Dumpling and Spaghetti still argue, but dine together.
- Sushi sits quietly, orders water.
